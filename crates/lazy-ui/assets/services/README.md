# Service logos

The marks of the services the Integrations dialog connects to (GitHub, Linear, Jira, Sentry,
PagerDuty), drawn in white on each service's color in `integration_ui::service_mark`.

The SVG files are from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) (`simple-icons` on npm), whose icon
data is released under CC0 1.0. The marks themselves are trademarks of their owners; Lazy shows
them only to identify the service a connector links to, as the owners' brand guidelines allow for
integrations. Do not recolor them other than to white on the brand color, stretch them, or use them
for anything else.
