LAZY / BRAND EXPLORATIONS / 2026

Why this direction

Warm, tactile and quietly friendly. The closest fit to the name Lazy: an easy place to talk, with a competent sloth as its companion.

Best for: Teams that want everyday work to feel more human.

Logo & illustration

A sloth face tucked into a rounded speech bubble. The low, curved eye patches connect the name to relaxed attention; the tail keeps the chat category clear.

Layered cut paper, organic silhouettes and subtle fiber texture. Keep texture in illustrations; keep interface surfaces clean.

Mascot role

An awake, reassuring sloth with a sage body, a cream face and a coral chat bubble. Moss welcomes people, explains empty states and celebrates small wins.

Design consideration: Keep Moss awake and the product copy direct so warmth does not suggest slow software.

LAZY / BRAND EXPLORATIONS / 2026

Why this direction

Bold, geometric and refreshingly direct. A continuous chat loop creates a recognizable mark, with a bright snail adding wit and patience.

Best for: Builders and small teams that like sharp, expressive tools.

Logo & illustration

A compact spiral with a speech-bubble tail. It combines a conversation loop with Pip's shell and holds a strong silhouette at small sizes.

Flat geometric forms, confident navy contours and light print grain. Use strong crops and one oversized shape per composition.

Mascot role

An alert lime snail with a cobalt spiral shell and a small speech-bubble tail. Pip suggests steady progress and keeps explanations light.

LAZY / BRAND EXPLORATIONS / 2026

Why this direction

Playful, expressive and full of character. Bold teal and coral, flowing forms and a sociable octopus make an approachable identity with plenty of illustration potential.

Best for: Friendly, collaborative teams that want their tools to have personality.

Logo & illustration

A rounded bubble crest above four flowing arms. It reduces Ocho's shape into a simple wave-and-conversation symbol rather than a detailed animal portrait.

Bold rounded ink contours, broad teal ribbons and light risograph grain. Keep artwork spacious and limit decorative shapes around product UI.

Mascot role

A teal octopus with curling arms and a coral conversation bubble. Ocho brings scattered conversations together and can support reactions, stickers and welcome states.

Design consideration: Keep the octopus in onboarding and marketing so the everyday workspace remains focused.
