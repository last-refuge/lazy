# Lazy — five brand directions

Created October 29, 2026. These are complete exploration kits for choosing an identity for Lazy, the native workplace chat app. Direction names are internal concept labels; the product name remains **Lazy**.

Open `index.html` for the comparison gallery. It works offline and includes one full brand board and implementation notes per direction. Use the direction buttons to focus on one option. The gallery is a local design artifact, not a published website.

## Recommendation

**Soft Signal** is the strongest overall fit for the name and a calmer approach to team chat. **Good Loop** has the strongest compact graphic mark. **Afterglow** is the strongest premium, dark-first direction.

## Soft Signal

Warm, tactile and quietly friendly. The closest fit to the name Lazy: an easy place to talk, with a competent sloth as its companion.

- **Mascot:** Moss, the paper-cut sloth.
- **Logo:** A sloth face tucked into a rounded speech bubble. The low, curved eye patches connect the name to relaxed attention; the tail keeps the chat category clear.
- **Illustration:** A canopy of speech-bubble leaves and a curved branch turns a conversation into a comfortable shared space.
- **Display font:** Nunito, weight 600–800.
- **Body/UI font:** DM Sans, weight 400 for copy and 600 for controls.
- **Main colors:** brand #183F35; background #F5F1E7; support #A9C3A0; accent #E9A38D.
- **Character:** Gentle · welcoming · grounded.
- **Best for:** Teams that want everyday work to feel more human.
- **Art direction:** Layered cut paper, organic silhouettes and subtle fiber texture. Keep texture in illustrations; keep interface surfaces clean.
- **Consideration:** Keep Moss awake and the product copy direct so warmth does not suggest slow software.

Files: `01-soft-signal/`. Main board: `brand-board.png`.

## Good Loop

Bold, geometric and refreshingly direct. A continuous chat loop creates a recognizable mark, with a bright snail adding wit and patience.

- **Mascot:** Pip, the cobalt-shell snail.
- **Logo:** A compact spiral with a speech-bubble tail. It combines a conversation loop with Pip's shell and holds a strong silhouette at small sizes.
- **Illustration:** A cobalt ribbon forms an open loop around a few conversations. Pip moves along the same line without urgency.
- **Display font:** Space Grotesk, weight 600–800.
- **Body/UI font:** IBM Plex Sans, weight 400 for copy and 600 for controls.
- **Main colors:** brand #2448D8; background #F7F8F1; support #AABAF2; accent #DAEF71.
- **Character:** Clever · crisp · upbeat.
- **Best for:** Builders and small teams that like sharp, expressive tools.
- **Art direction:** Flat geometric forms, confident navy contours and light print grain. Use strong crops and one oversized shape per composition.
- **Consideration:** Use lime as a highlight and Pip sparingly so the app stays visually calm.

Files: `02-good-loop/`. Main board: `brand-board.png`.

## Afterglow

A quiet, premium identity with a midnight foundation, lilac light and tactile ceramic illustrations. A moon moth gives the system an unexpected companion.

- **Mascot:** Luma, the ceramic moon moth.
- **Logo:** A crescent with a small chat tail and a separate point of light. Its open center makes the mark feel spacious and gives it a clear dark-background silhouette.
- **Illustration:** An oversized ceramic crescent cradles glowing conversation bubbles while Luma floats through the open space.
- **Display font:** Sora, weight 600–800.
- **Body/UI font:** IBM Plex Sans, weight 400 for copy and 600 for controls.
- **Main colors:** brand #C1B8F4; background #19182C; support #39344F; accent #F3B69F.
- **Character:** Composed · thoughtful · luminous.
- **Best for:** Design-conscious teams that value focused work and a polished desktop experience.
- **Art direction:** Matte ceramic, soft volumetric light and generous negative space. Reserve glow and 3D rendering for marketing art.
- **Consideration:** Balance night imagery with daytime product copy so it does not imply working after hours.

Files: `03-afterglow/`. Main board: `brand-board.png`.

## Common Ground

Warm editorial typography, earthen color and hand-printed illustrations. The identity makes team chat feel like a shared place rather than a stream of demands.

- **Mascot:** Cora, the linocut capybara.
- **Logo:** Two neighboring arches connected at a shared stem, ending in a conversation tail. The mark suggests people finding common ground.
- **Illustration:** Interlocking ochre and terracotta arches create a communal courtyard, with conversation bubbles and Cora among olive leaves.
- **Display font:** Fraunces, weight 600–800.
- **Body/UI font:** DM Sans, weight 400 for copy and 600 for controls.
- **Main colors:** brand #A54832; background #F6EFDF; support #4B5B3B; accent #D3A44C.
- **Character:** Companionable · considered · down to earth.
- **Best for:** Close-knit creative teams, studios and communities.
- **Art direction:** Dry-brush edges, linocut grain, architectural arches and generous cream paper. Pair expressive headlines with plain, highly readable interface text.
- **Consideration:** Use the serif only for brand moments and headings; dense chat interfaces should stay in DM Sans.

Files: `04-common-ground/`. Main board: `brand-board.png`.

## Easy Tide

Playful, expressive and full of character. Bold teal and coral, flowing forms and a sociable octopus make an approachable identity with plenty of illustration potential.

- **Mascot:** Ocho, the screenprint octopus.
- **Logo:** A rounded bubble crest above four flowing arms. It reduces Ocho's shape into a simple wave-and-conversation symbol rather than a detailed animal portrait.
- **Illustration:** Broad wave ribbons form a circular current as Ocho gently gathers coral chat bubbles into one shared flow.
- **Display font:** Bricolage Grotesque, weight 600–800.
- **Body/UI font:** DM Sans, weight 400 for copy and 600 for controls.
- **Main colors:** brand #087A73; background #FFF3DF; support #A5D8CF; accent #F28C78.
- **Character:** Sociable · buoyant · resourceful.
- **Best for:** Friendly, collaborative teams that want their tools to have personality.
- **Art direction:** Bold rounded ink contours, broad teal ribbons and light risograph grain. Keep artwork spacious and limit decorative shapes around product UI.
- **Consideration:** Keep the octopus in onboarding and marketing so the everyday workspace remains focused.

Files: `05-easy-tide/`. Main board: `brand-board.png`.

## Shared website specification

- **Layout:** 1200 px maximum content width; 24 px mobile gutters and 48–64 px desktop gutters. Use a 4 px spacing unit; section spacing 64 px mobile and 112 px desktop.
- **Type scale:** desktop display 64–80 px / 1.05, mobile display 40–48 px / 1.1; h2 36–48 px / 1.15; h3 24–28 px / 1.25; body 16–18 px / 1.55; controls 14–16 px / 1.2; captions at least 12 px / 1.4. Use display fonts for brand headlines, body fonts for navigation, product copy and chat.
- **Buttons:** 44 px minimum height; 16–24 px horizontal padding; 600 weight. Use brand/on-brand as a pair. Use the supplied per-direction corner radius. Focus treatment: 3 px visible outline with a 3 px offset.
- **Borders:** one pixel. Use text color for essential controls when a decorative border token is too subtle. Avoid shadows except for layered dialogs and small product surfaces.
- **Motion:** 160 ms ease-out for state changes; respect reduced motion. Keep mascot motion occasional and avoid looping in core work areas.
- **Icons:** one consistent rounded outline icon family, 20 or 24 px, 1.75–2 px stroke. Logo files are original editable SVG paths; wordmarks are outlined and do not depend on installed fonts.
- **Logo usage:** primary horizontal lockup in navigation; symbol for app icons and tight spaces. Minimum 96 px horizontal lockup width, 24 px standalone symbol, 16 px favicon. Keep clear space equal to one quarter of the symbol width. Use the monochrome/reverse variant when needed; do not stretch, rotate or add effects.
- **Illustration:** use the landscape illustration in marketing sections; use the separate transparent mascot in welcome, empty and success states. Keep actual text outside raster artwork. Do not replace a product screenshot with a fictional illustration. The chat examples in the gallery are styling samples, not screenshots of the app.
- **Voice:** direct, warm and useful. Say “Message your team”, “You’re all caught up” and “Try again”. Keep wordplay in marketing headlines and remove it from errors and security-related copy.
- **Color semantics:** brand colors do not automatically indicate success, warning or error. Pair status with a label/icon and independently verify those future status colors. Use the tested text pairs recorded in each `brand.json`.
- **Themes:** per-direction `tokens.css` includes dark-system overrides. Afterglow starts in its dark palette; a light version would be a subsequent extension. Keep the foreground and background token pairs together.

## Assets and implementation

Each direction includes `logo.svg`, `logo-reversed.svg`, `logo-mono.svg`, `mark.svg`, `mark-mono.svg`, `favicon.svg`, `mascot.png`, `mascot.webp`, `illustration.png`, `illustration.webp`, `tokens.css`, `brand.json` and `brand-board.png`.

Import the chosen `tokens.css` and preserve the relative `fonts.css` and `fonts/` paths, or update their URLs when moving assets into a website. Use SVG for marks and wordmarks, WebP for website illustrations, and the transparent PNG originals for further art work. The favicon can also serve as the starting artwork for platform-specific application icon exports.

Raster artwork was generated with the built-in **image_gen** tool. `generation-manifest.json` contains the exact mascot and illustration prompt set, reference paths and source-image paths. Mascots have true RGBA transparency. SVG logos were drawn as vector geometry and font outlines. Generated artwork is exploratory.

## Rebuilding

Run `prepare-fonts.py` only to refresh upstream font sources and install temporary font tooling; it requires network access and Python with pip. Run `build.py` with Pillow and fontTools available to rebuild the gallery, vector wordmarks, WebP assets and tokens. `render-gallery.cjs` uses Playwright to render the five PNG boards and verify the review controls at desktop and mobile widths.
