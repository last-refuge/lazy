06 / Illustration language

Conversation grows here.

Large, organic, tactile.

Layered cut paper. Speech-bubble leaves. Gentle curved branches. A small Apricot accent.

Keep texture in artwork and clean surfaces in UI. Place live text beside the illustration. Stack the whole scene below the headline on mobile.

Hero master: 1536 × 1024.
WebP: 480, 960 and 1536 px wide.
Repeating vector pattern: 480 × 320.

Use the pattern as a quiet accent on covers and decorative areas. Keep it away from reading text, forms and message transcripts. Do not stretch or recolor the raster master.
